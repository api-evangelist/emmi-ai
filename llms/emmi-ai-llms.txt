# Emmi AI

> Emmi AI is an Austrian engineering-AI company building Large Engineering Models (LEMs) - pre-trained, physics-accurate neural networks that replace traditional CAE/CFD solvers to deliver real-time, GPU-accelerated simulation and design validation for manufacturing, aerospace, semiconductor, and energy engineering. Its open-source Noether framework (the emmiai-noether PyTorch package) provides transformer building blocks, a model/dataset/recipe zoo, and a CLI for training and deploying industrial physics models. Emmi AI was acquired by Mistral AI in May 2026.

This file was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the repository catalog (apis.yml + repo artifacts). Emmi AI publishes no REST/OpenAPI HTTP API; the product surface is the open-source Noether Python framework and CLI.

## Products
- [Noether Framework](https://github.com/Emmi-AI/noether): Open-source PyTorch deep-learning framework for Engineering AI (transformer building blocks, model/dataset/recipe zoo).
- [NeuralWing](https://emmi.ai): Real-time aircraft wing design validation.
- [NeuralMould](https://emmi.ai): Digital engineer for injection moulding.
- [NeuralDEM](https://github.com/Emmi-AI/NeuralDEM): Real-time simulation of industrial particulate flows.
